HOW THIS WORKS

A useful estimate, not your payslip.

Your annual salary is spread evenly across Monday to Friday workdays in the Australian financial year. Today advances only during your chosen work hours, excluding your break. Paid leave and public holidays count as ordinary weekdays; bonuses, deductions, HELP repayments and tax offsets are not included. If your package includes super, the selected rate is used to estimate the salary portion.

Tax uses the ATO resident income tax rates, the 2% Medicare levy, 2026–27 surcharge tiers, and optional Division 293. Division 293 is deducted from this estimate even if you pay it from super. The annual estimate is allocated evenly as work is done; it does not model payroll withholding dates.
